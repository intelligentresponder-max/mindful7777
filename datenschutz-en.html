3. Hosting and server logs

This website is hosted as a static HTML site. Depending on the hosting provider, server log files may be collected that contain the following data:

  • IP address of the accessing computer
  • Date and time of access
  • Name and URL of the file requested
  • Amount of data transferred
  • Browser type and version
  • Operating system

These data are stored for technical reasons and to ensure trouble-free operation (legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). The data are deleted after 7 days at the latest.

Purpose and scope

If you contact us by e-mail, for example to request an offer or to place an order, we process your e-mail address and the content you provide in order to handle your request and to send you the requested materials by e-mail.

Legal basis

Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (contract or pre-contractual measures) or Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in answering inquiries). The data are deleted as soon as they are no longer required for processing and no statutory retention obligations apply.

Throne Computer Corporation
Registered office: USA

Throne processes the data of gift-givers (name, e-mail, payment information, shipping address) under its own responsibility. As the website operator I have no influence on data processing by Throne.

Legal basis

Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest). The legitimate interest lies in offering visitors a way to send gifts without my private address becoming known.

8. Login function (sessionStorage)

Purpose and scope

The password-protected member area of this website uses sessionStorage (a browser feature) to store the login status. These data are stored exclusively locally in your browser and are not transmitted to servers.

Data stored

  • A login flag (e.g. mf7777_auth = 1)
  • No passwords, no personal data

Storage period

The data are deleted automatically as soon as you close the browser tab (sessionStorage is not persistent).

Legal basis

Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest). The legitimate interest lies in the technical provision of the password-protected area.

9. Onboarding questionnaire (sending by e-mail)

Purpose and scope

The interactive onboarding questionnaire serves to arrange a session. The information you provide in the form — including the answers on health-related points in step 7 — is sent only if you actively trigger sending (via your e-mail program or the copy function). There is no automatic, server-side storage of the entries.

Recipient

Sending is made to the mail account of mindful7777 (intelligent.responder@gmail.com). Your own e-mail program handles sending — the information does not pass through any further third-party providers.

Legal basis

Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR (consent) and, where health-related information is concerned, Art. 9(2)(a) GDPR (explicit consent to special categories of personal data). Consent is given by actively filling in and submitting the form.

Withdrawal

You can withdraw your consent at any time, without formalities, by e-mail to intelligent.responder@gmail.com. The lawfulness of processing carried out before the withdrawal remains unaffected.

Restriction of processing (Art. 18 GDPR)

You can request that the processing of your data be restricted.

You have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority. The competent authority is that of your place of residence or stay, or that of the controller.
